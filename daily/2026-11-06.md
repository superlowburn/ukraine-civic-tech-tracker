# Ukraine Civic Tech — Friday, November 6, 2026

Covers items published from Thursday morning, November 5, to Friday morning, November 6, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[Defense tech] Ukrainian arms makers say the new 20–30% export fee "eats all the margin."** Under Cabinet resolution No. 875, companies pay the state 20% of an export contract's value for finished products and technologies, and 30% for components, upfront and non-refundable if a permit is refused. AIN spoke with Dwarf Engineering, BlueBird Tech, the Gunsmiths Council and the Technological Forces of Ukraine association. Five of its six industry sources called the fee unaffordable, especially for small first contracts abroad. Proposals range from a 0% rate for newcomers or joint ventures to a 10% rate. ([AIN](https://ain.ua/2026/11/05/zyidaje-vsiu-marzu-comu-ukrayinskim-virobnikam-nevigidno-eksportuvati-zbroiu/))
- **[Drones] Phantom Defense is preparing its Mangal anti-drone protection system for mass production.** The system is designed to automatically catch attacking FPV drones before they hit vehicles, ground robots or boats. The Ukrainian company says ten units are with frontline units for combat testing, that they have repelled two FPV attacks, and that a second version is in development. These are the developer's own claims. ([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/ukrainian-mangal-active-protection-system/))
- **[Drones] Swarmer's shareholders clear the share issue for its planned purchase of Ukrainian ground-robot maker Ratel Robotics.** At a November 2 meeting, reported on November 5, they approved up to 5.5 million new shares, about a third of those outstanding. Most of the shares depend on Ratel meeting performance targets. Ukrainian media value the deal at up to $224 million. ([Interfax-Ukraine](https://interfax.com.ua/news/telecom/1231502.html), [dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/swarmer-kupuie-ratel-robotics-1793791752))
- **[Drones] Ukrainian FPV maker WinFly and Taiwanese boatbuilder Fortmosa are designing an uncrewed boat that carries interceptor drones.** The boat is meant to protect coastal waters. It is still at the concept and mock-up stage: Fortmosa will build a prototype and WinFly will add autonomy and drones. ([LB.ua](https://lb.ua/tech/2026/11/05/778826_ukrayinska_winfly_tayvanska.html))
- **[Defense tech / AI] South Korean startup Newtype Industries says it has stopped testing its AI artillery software with Ukrainian forces.** Its CEO blames the diplomatic row between Seoul and Kyiv over two North Korean prisoners of war. The Diplomat, as cited by Mind.ua, notes there is no official confirmation of that link. The company has also stopped looking for Ukrainian partners for now. ([Mind.ua](https://mind.ua/news/20311887-pivdennokorejskij-oboronnij-startap-pripinyae-diyalnist-v-ukrayini))
- **[Cyber] Ukraine has faced nearly 17,000 cyberattacks since the full-scale invasion, an SBU cyber official tells the Tallinn Digital Summit.** He said most were stopped early and most are linked to Russian special services. Forbes Ukraine set the figure alongside this week's hacks of the Air Alert app and the Kyiv Independent. The SBU has not attributed those two incidents. ([Forbes Ukraine](https://forbes.ua/news/rosiiski-specsluzbi-zdiisnili-proti-ukrayini-maize-17-000-kiberatak-iz-2022-roku-sbu-05112026-43547))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on several fronts:

- IT Army or other hacktivist claims.
- InformNapalm, DeepState or KSE Institute publications.
- Diia or Digital Ministry launches.
- New campaigns from the major foundations (Come Back Alive, Prytula, Sternenko, UNITED24).

## Worth watching

- **Export rules vs. joint ventures:** Industry sources tell AIN that a 0% fee for joint ventures is under discussion. Watch whether the Cabinet changes resolution No. 875, since many makers now produce abroad through joint ventures to get around direct-export limits.
- **Attacks on civilian information channels:** Still no published audit results or formal attribution for the Air Alert or Kyiv Independent hacks.
