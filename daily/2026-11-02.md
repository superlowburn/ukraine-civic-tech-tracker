# Ukraine Civic Tech — Monday, November 2, 2026

Covers items published from Saturday morning, October 31, to Monday morning, November 2, 2026 (Kyiv time). The weekend was dominated by one story: Ukraine's main public air-raid alert app was hacked. Only three genuinely new items made the cut. Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[Civilian apps / Cyber] Ukraine's "Air Alert" app was hacked late Sunday and sent false warnings across the country.** Users got fake alerts about radiation, a chemical threat and street fighting in Kyiv. The Air Force and the Center for Countering Disinformation said there was no such threat. The app was built in 2022 by Ajax Systems with the Digital Transformation Ministry. It was taken offline, and by Monday morning it was being restored with the ministry's help. Air Force spokesman Yurii Ihnat said the app is separate from military systems and defence operations were not affected. The ministry told people to rely on street sirens, their phones' built-in emergency alerts, the Air Force's official messenger channels and the Kyiv Digital app. A group calling itself Hitcore claimed the attack through a Russian Telegram channel. That claim has not been independently verified. ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraines-air-raid-alert-system-hacked-air-force-says-as-false-nuclear-chemical-warnings-sound), [AP via WRAL](https://www.wral.com/news/ap/71737-ukraine-and-russia-both-report-cyberattacks-on-emergency-apps/), [Apostrophe](https://apostrophe.ua/society/zlamali-zastosunok-povitrjana-trivoha-jurij-ihnat-rozpoviv-shcho-naspravdi-stalosja-vnochi.html), [my.ua](https://my.ua/uk/news/cluster/2026-11-02-kiberataka-na-zastosunok-povitriana-trivoga-zaiava-mintsifri-ta-zaiava-khakeriv-shcho-robiti))
- **[Cyber] Hours later, Russia's emergency ministry app also sent fake alerts, and the pro-Ukrainian partisan movement ATESH claims its hackers did it.** On the night of November 1–2, a fake message on the Russian app claimed nuclear warheads had been launched at Russian regions. Russia's Emergencies Ministry confirmed a "massive cyberattack" on its app. It said the rest of its warning system, including SMS and sirens, kept working. The ministry did not say who was behind the attack. ATESH's claim is **self-reported and unverified**, and Kyiv has not said Ukraine was involved. ([UNN](https://unn.ua/en/news/after-fake-alerts-in-ukraine-someone-hacked-russias-emergency-situations-ministry-app-and-announced-the-threat-of-a-nuclear-strike-against-the-russian-federation-atesh), [Ukrainska Pravda via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/russian-air-alert-application-hacked-024500010.html), [Euronews](https://euronews.com/2026/11/02/ukraine-and-russia-say-air-raid-alert-apps-hit-by-cyberattacks-after-truce-ends))
- **[Drones] An upgraded ZIRKA interceptor from Ukrainian company NOCTIS reportedly shot down one of Russia's jet-powered Geran-5 drones.** The Geran-5 was flying at about 400 km/h. ZRK.tech, the project behind the interceptor, announced the result, and Militarnyi reported it on Sunday. Drone maker Vyriy Industries, which invests in NOCTIS, published the video. The result is the company's own claim. It adds to the race among Ukrainian startups to build cheap interceptors against jet Shaheds. ([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/upgraded-zirka-interceptor-drone-destroys-geran-5-at-400-km-h/))

## Worth watching

- **Securing civilian alert apps:** Watch for a technical post-mortem or official attribution from the Digital Ministry, Ajax or CERT-UA, and for any changes to how the many civilian-built alert tools are secured. Russia has now shown it can turn one of Ukraine's most-used civic apps into a panic channel.
- **Brave1 grant deadline:** Applications for the radar, air-to-air missile and turbojet engine grants close November 5.
