# Ukraine Civic Tech — Monday, November 9, 2026

Covers items published from Saturday morning, November 7, to Monday morning, November 9, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out. The weekend was quiet, and most new items are interviews and product updates.

## Top items

- **[Crowdfunding] The Serhiy Prytula Charity Foundation says a Ukrainian-made alternative to Starlink for drone links is now in combat trials.** This follows up on the project first mentioned on November 7. CEO Andriy Shuvalov told AIN it is a domestically produced radio link, not a satellite network. The foundation wants several manufacturers to be able to use it. It plans a large fundraiser to buy the system only after the trials confirm it works, ideally by the end of the year or in January. The foundation has not published any test results yet. ([AIN](https://ain.ua/2026/11/08/v-ukrayini-vze-testuiut-alternativu-starlink-na-yiyi-zakupivliu-ogolosiat-velikii-zbir-anonsuvali-u-fondi-sergiia-prituli/))
- **[Drones] Prytula's CEO says Ukraine has again fallen behind Russia's jet-powered Shahed drones.** In a second AIN interview, Shuvalov said today's interceptor drones are barely as fast as the jet drones they chase. He sees cheap short-range air-defense missiles, at about $20,000 each, as the real answer, but says no maker can yet supply them in bulk. The foundation recently delivered 626 ZIRKA interceptors to the Armed Forces and National Guard. It is funding them through its combined "Yedynozbir. Chyste Nebo" ("Clear Sky") campaign. ([AIN](https://ain.ua/2026/11/09/iakii-zasib-potriben-ukrayini-dlia-efektivnoyi-protidiyi-reaktivnim-saxedam-interviu-z-andrijem-suvalovim-ocilnikom-fondu-sergiia-prituli/), [Defence Express](https://defence-ua.com/news/sche_626_droniv_zirka_jaki_vzhe_zbivajut_rosijski_droni_geran_5_otrimali_zsu_ta_ngu_vid_fondu_sergija_prituli-24647.html))
- **[AI] The Digital Transformation Ministry is recruiting volunteers to help train Syaivo, Ukraine's national language model.** Volunteers check computer-read text from scanned archival documents against the originals and fix errors. No technical skills are needed, and people sign up through a short online form. The State Archives have handed over 10 terabytes of material. More than 50 partners, including universities, libraries and media outlets, are contributing data. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/siaivo-navchannia-1794040322))
- **[Drones] Ground-robot maker DevDroid says its robots can now keep going when they lose their Starlink connection.** The updated control system lets a robot keep moving on its own through spots without coverage, and bring itself back if the link doesn't return. The company also says it has delivered robots with thermal cameras to the military. dev.ua based its report on Oboronka, and the claims have not been independently confirmed. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/droid-box-onovlennia-1794043548))
- **[Defense tech] Ukrainian firm Abacus-Tech is testing an anti-drone jamming system that switches on and off by itself.** Most such systems need an operator to turn them on. This one reacts automatically to threat warnings from connected detection systems. The company says this avoids jamming friendly drones and saves power. These are the developer's own claims, and the system is still in testing. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/namet-8-1794134628))
- **[Defense tech] Venture fund WildField Ventures has quietly invested about $750,000 in two Ukrainian defense-tech startups.** Partner Maksym Zubenko told AIN that one startup got $250,000 and the other $500,000. Both are at pre-seed or seed stage, and the fund plans to name them by the end of 2026. The fund targets $15 million and expects talks with three to five more startups in early 2027. In a companion piece, Zubenko said a foreign defense-tech startup can cost up to five times more than a comparable Ukrainian one. ([AIN](https://ain.ua/2026/11/09/wildfield-ventures-investuvav-750-000-u-dva-ukrayinski-defense-tech-startapi-rozpoviv-partner-fondu-ain/), [AIN](https://ain.ua/2026/11/08/inozemnii-defense-tech-startap-moze-kostuvati-vpiatero-dorozce-za-ukrayinskii-analog-partner-fondu-wildfield-ventures/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on several fronts:

- IT Army or other hacktivist claims.
- InformNapalm, DeepState or KSE Institute publications, beyond those covered on November 8.
- New Diia services.
- New campaigns from Come Back Alive, the Sternenko foundation or UNITED24.

## Worth watching

- **The race to replace Starlink:** Ukrainian intelligence said on November 7 that Russia's own satellite system, Rassvet, is already working over Ukraine. Meanwhile, Prytula's foundation and DevDroid are both working to reduce Ukraine's reliance on Starlink. Watch for the trial results and the fundraiser for Prytula's system, expected around the New Year.
- **Air Alert and Kyiv Independent hacks:** Still no published audit or formal attribution.
