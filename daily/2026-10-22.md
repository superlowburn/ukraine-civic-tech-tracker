# Ukraine Civic Tech — Thursday, October 22, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Wednesday morning, October 21, to Thursday morning, October 22, 2026 (Kyiv time).

## Top items

- **[Drones] Volunteer group DroneAid is building a net-based shield against FPV drones.** The international volunteer initiative showed its DRONOLAW active-protection system, which is meant to physically catch attacking FPV drones, including fibre-optic ones that jamming can't stop. After about 18 months of development and early tests abroad, the team is preparing trials at a Ukrainian military training ground. It says it won't move to front-line use until the system meets a minimum interception rate it has set. Performance figures are the developers' own. ([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/droneaid-volunteers-develop-dronolaw-active-protection-system-against-fpv-drones/), [dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/droneaid-1792644841))
- **[Drones] Wild Hornets, a volunteer-founded interceptor maker, is reducing its dependence on Starlink.** Following last week's Starshield speed restrictions, the maker of the Sting interceptor said its current operations are unaffected. It is still reconsidering some projects, such as launching interceptors from aircraft, and is testing other ways to connect. A spokesman warned that relying on one solution is very risky. The company says it is developing its own video-and-control system, and that the Defence Ministry has given it private air-defence status. ([Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/87732), [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/10/21/starlink-slowdown-makes-ukraines-shahed-hunting-drone-maker-rethink-air-launched-interceptors/))
- **[Drones] The defence-industry council says Ukraine's arms-export mechanism still isn't working, a year on.** Ihor Fedirko, CEO of the Ukrainian Council of Defence Industry, said eight joint ventures have been set up abroad and five supply Ukraine. He said the special export mechanism isn't yet working effectively and the "Build with Ukraine" rules are unclear. He wants lower fees, simpler test batches, and public data on what exports have brought to defence. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/export-delays-fedirko/))
- **[Drones] Lastivka strike drones are now listed on the Brave1 Market.** The maker says its fixed-wing drone line is already in combat use and is waiting to be added to the DOT-Chain Defence marketplace. Units will be able to order it with Army of Drones ePoints. Combat-use claims are the manufacturer's own. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/lastivka-kamikadze/))
- **[Drones] Defence-tech firms keep fighting a cut to staff draft deferrals.** Defender Media's weekly roundup says companies including The Fourth Law/Odd Systems, Ukrspecsystems and General Cherry oppose the government's plan to cut the share of critical defence-company staff who can be deferred from 100% to 85%. A parliamentary resolution to block the plan has gathered dozens of MPs' signatures. The final government decision hadn't been published as of the roundup. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/dorozvidka-82/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on volunteer cyber/OSINT groups (InformNapalm's latest English publication is still the October 19 Babakov investigation), civilian alert/AI apps or Diia, KSE sanctions tracking, or the major foundations (Come Back Alive, Prytula, Sternenko, UNITED24).

## Worth watching

- **Satellite-link resilience:** the Starshield limits are pushing civilian-founded drone makers such as Wild Hornets to find alternatives, including European networks. Watch whether foundations and Brave1 start funding Starlink-independent communications.
- **Workforce and exports:** the fights over deferral quotas and export rules are both about whether Ukraine's fast-growing drone industry can keep its staff and its capital. A final government decision on deferrals is expected soon.
