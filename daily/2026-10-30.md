# Ukraine Civic Tech — Friday, October 30, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Thursday morning, October 29, to Friday morning, October 30, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[Drones] Brave1 says automated turrets in a private air-defence project have shot down dozens of Russian drones.** Defender Media reports that the turrets, part of a project run by private air-defence companies working with the Air Force, have destroyed more than 60 targets, including jet-powered drones. The figures come from Brave1, the government's defence-tech cluster, and an Air Force spokesperson. They have not been independently verified, and the article's own numbers are not fully consistent. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/turret-for-air-defence/))
- **[Cyber] Digital Minister Oksana Ferchuk says Russia is using AI to speed up cyberattacks on Ukrainian government bodies and businesses.** Speaking at the TECHNOVATION event during the Kyiv Economic Forum, she said public bodies should finish forming their own cyber teams in the coming weeks. Every business should have a named security specialist or team, and large firms should run professional monitoring tools on staff computers. The ministry also plans to invest in cybersecurity and AI education. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/ferchuk-1793282065), [Interfax-Ukraine](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/telecom/1227319.html))
- **[Counter-disinformation] Ukraine's Center for Countering Disinformation flags AI-made videos in Poland that falsely show Ukrainian local councillors insulting Poles.** The centre says the short clips are spreading on TikTok, Facebook and X, use AI avatars, and match no real posts by the officials. It says the campaign is still running and points to signs of Russian origin. These are the centre's own findings. ([UNN](https://unn.ua/en/news/ai-generated-videos-are-being-circulated-in-poland-to-stoke-anti-ukrainian-sentiment-cpd))
- **[Cyber] Ukraine's State Special Communications Service warns of phishing emails sent in DTEK's name.** The service says a wave of emails on October 29 told state institutions they owed money for electricity, with the aim of getting staff to install remote-access software. It linked the wave to a known hacker group, UAC-0050. It urged people to check bills only through official provider apps or verified portals. ([LB.ua](https://lb.ua/tech/2026/10/30/777278_hakeri_rozsilayut_listi.html))
- **[Civilian apps] Mobile operators have bought 13,000 generators to keep base stations running through blackouts.** The Digital Ministry says it is working with operators and local governments on a system to switch the generators on during outages. Ferchuk said owning generators does not guarantee service: they also need fuel, maintenance and to start on time. The ministry is also encouraging households to switch to fibre (xPON) connections that can stay online on backup power. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/heneratory-1793282804))
- **[Drones] Ukraine's Roboneers and Germany's ARX Robotics finish early integration tests of a Ukrainian combat module on a German ground robot.** The companies aim to complete the integration by the end of 2026, and the first units are meant for Ukraine's Defence Forces. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/gereon-saber-integrity/), [dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/arx-robotics-ta-roboneers-intehruiut-boiovyi-modul-shablia-z-nrk-gereon-1793275032))
- **[Defense tech] Ukrainian electronic-warfare maker Unwave signs a cooperation memorandum with Canada's 49North, part of MDA Space.** The companies plan to produce and maintain counter-drone systems together under the "Build with Ukraine" programme. They have not said which systems they will make or when production will start. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/unwave-ta-49north-1793269479))
- **[Drones] Drone maker Vyriy Industries says its flight school has trained more than 500 military personnel on its Sokil reconnaissance drone.** That includes more than 170 crews since March. The company says it has added six partner training centres, and training is included in the drone's price. These are the company's own figures. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/vyriy-aero-school-1793290248))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on several fronts:

- IT Army or other Ukrainian volunteer hacker groups' claims.
- New InformNapalm investigations; DeepState kept up routine map updates.
- KSE Institute's sanctions and shadow-fleet trackers.
- New campaigns from Prytula, Sternenko, Come Back Alive or UNITED24.
- The final Dronopad total from the Radio Dictation fundraiser, which still hasn't been published.

## Worth watching

- **AI on both sides of the information fight:** In one day, officials reported AI-assisted Russian cyberattacks, AI-made fake videos in Poland and a phishing wave against state bodies. Watch whether the government's new cyber-team deadline for public bodies is met, and how Ukraine's own AI defences are used.
- **Private air defence and foreign partnerships:** Private turret operators, Nova Poshta's air-defence team and new deals with German and Canadian firms point to civilian companies taking a bigger role in protecting the country.
