# Ukraine Civic Tech — Sunday, November 1, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Saturday morning, October 31, to Sunday morning, November 1, 2026 (Kyiv time). It was a quiet weekend window, so this brief has only three items. One of them, from Friday evening, was missed by Saturday's brief. Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[OSINT] The Sahaidachnyi Security Center has mapped 129 drone incidents in 18 EU countries since 2022.** Romania has the most incidents (35) and Germany is next (23). The figures come from the Ukrainian think tank's interactive map of threats to Europe, which is built from open sources. Director Lesya Ogryzko called the map "the tip of the iceberg" and said many incidents go unreported. Speaking at the same Kyiv event, she also argued that Ukraine gets less from its partnership with Palantir than the US company gets from Ukraine's battlefield data. She called for Ukraine and Europe to build sovereign AI together. ([dev.ua — incidents](https://dev.ua/en/news/129-dronovykh-intsydentiv-1793442813), [dev.ua — Palantir](https://dev.ua/en/news/ekspertka-poiasnyla-chomu-palantir-pylososyt-ukrainu-i-sam-otrymuie-vid-spivpratsi-nabahato-bilshe-nizh-viddaie-1793451311))
- **[Defense tech] The CEO of Ukrainian electronic-warfare maker UNWAVE tells Europe that defending against drones should cost about as much as the drones.** In an interview with Ukrinform, Taras Bublyk argued for layered defences that combine detection, electronic warfare, interceptor drones and physical protection. Expensive missiles would be kept for when cheaper options fail. He also urged European states to clear legal hurdles to using electronic warfare quickly in emergencies. This is the company's own view, and one republication (Euromaidan Press) labels it industry advertorial content. ([Ukrinform](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-defense/4180045-cost-of-countering-aerial-threats-should-be-proportionate-to-threat-defense-industry-representative.html), [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/10/31/european-countries-need-to-learn-ukrainian-defense-tech-ceo-warns-europe-against-costly-defense-mistakes/))
- **[Counter-disinformation] Ukrainian OSINT firm Terrecon says it found nearly 1,000 AI-made Russian propaganda songs with more than 86 million streams.** CEO Darya Verbytska told Forbes Ukraine that such tracks are sold as a cheap commissioned service. Fifteen of the 25 Russian creators Terrecon tested with fake orders agreed to make them. The firm says the songs are made with Western AI music tools, reached through intermediaries, and that moderation on Western platforms let some through. These are Terrecon's own findings. Published Friday evening (Oct 30) and missed by Saturday's brief. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/muzychna-ai-propahanda-rosii-1793375666))

## Worth watching

- **Ukraine selling its counter-drone know-how to Europe:** In the past week, a Ukrainian-led Drone Security Index, the Sahaidachnyi incident map and pitches from firms like UNWAVE all made the case that Europe is underprepared for drones. With the EU's drone hubs and Defence Industry Pact in planning, watch for concrete contracts rather than commentary.
- **Brave1 grant deadline:** Applications for the radar, air-to-air missile and turbojet engine grants close November 5.
