# Ukraine Civic Tech — Sunday, October 25, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Saturday morning, October 24, to Sunday morning, October 25, 2026 (Kyiv time). It was a quiet weekend window, so there are fewer items than usual.

## Top items

- **[Drones] Starlink and Starshield are back to full speed, the government confirms.** President Zelensky and the Defence Ministry said on October 24 that Starshield, the military version of Starlink, again works at speeds above 400 km/h and that the connection is stable. The speed limits imposed in mid-October had been disrupting drone and interceptor work. Volunteer-founded drone makers and foundations had loudly complained about the limits, and they had disagreed over whether the restrictions were over (covered October 24). ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/starshield-operations-restored-above-400-km-h-ukraines-defense-ministry-says/), [UNITED24 Media](https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/starlink-is-back-at-400-kmh-as-ukraine-breaks-through-speed-restrictions-after-high-level-talks-23769))
- **[Drones] Volunteer Serhii Sternenko and adviser "Flash" dispute Musk's claim that Ukraine broke Starlink's terms.** Elon Musk said Ukrainian forces had used civilian Starlink for offensive drone strikes and that this "loophole" had been closed. Sternenko, a volunteer, foundation head and former defence-ministry adviser, said satellite-dependent drone missions could only run on the military Starshield system, so civilian Starlink couldn't have been used. Serhii "Flash" Beskrestnov said the speed limits on civilian terminals since winter, and the fact that Starlink doesn't work over Russia, make that kind of use impossible. These are their own accounts, and Musk has not given details. ([ZN.ua](https://zn.ua/ukr/war/mask-zajaviv-shcho-ukrajina-porushila-pravila-vikoristannja-starlink-sternenko-i-flesh-zaperechili.html), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/starshield-operations-restored-above-400-km-h-ukraines-defense-ministry-says/))
- **[Drones] Germany is in talks to buy Ukraine's Bars jet-powered drones.** A German official told the Kyiv Independent that the talks are part of Germany's plan to buy deep-strike weapons from European producers. The drones' maker isn't publicly named, for security reasons. The two countries already agreed in July to produce Bars together under the "Build with Ukraine" initiative. Citing a September Kyiv School of Economics report, the article says Germany is Ukraine's biggest defence-tech partner, with 36 joint ventures. ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/germany-in-talks-to-buy-ukrainian-jet-powered-drones/), [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/24/8059517/))
- **[Drones] Defence-tech hiring hits a recent high, with 380+ openings in Defender Media's weekly roundup.** Drone maker Vyriy Industries alone lists about 300 openings. DevDroid, UFORCE, Fire Point, Tulip Tech and others are also hiring. That is up from 120+ in the October 3 edition, though the weekly totals swing. The jump comes right after the government decided to cap staff draft deferrals for defence companies below 100% (covered October 24). ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/defence-tech-jobs-81/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on volunteer cyber or OSINT groups (IT Army, Ukrainian Cyber Alliance, InformNapalm, DeepState) or KSE Institute's sanctions and shadow-fleet tracking. The same goes for civilian alert or AI apps, Diia, and new campaigns by the major foundations (Come Back Alive, Prytula, Sternenko, UNITED24).

## Worth watching

- **Radio Dictation fundraiser, Tuesday, October 27:** public broadcaster Suspilne will raise money for Come Back Alive's "Dronopad" drone-based air-defence project during this year's National Unity Radio Dictation. No target was announced ([Suspilne](https://suspilne.media/culture/1433745-pid-cas-radiodiktantu-suspilne-zbiratime-kosti-na-sistemu-ppo-dla-zbitta-rosijskih-droniv)).
- **Starlink after the restoration:** speeds are back, but Musk's accusation and US lawmakers' questions to him ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/us-lawmakers-demand-answers-from-elon-musk-over-ukraine-starlink-restrictions/)) leave the arrangement fragile. Watch whether volunteers' push for Starlink-independent links keeps its momentum.
