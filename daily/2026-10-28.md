# Ukraine Civic Tech — Wednesday, October 28, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Tuesday morning, October 27, to Wednesday morning, October 28, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[Crowdfunding] The Radio Dictation fundraiser for Come Back Alive's "Dronopad" passes about UAH 1 million.** Ukrainians wrote the National Unity Radio Dictation on Tuesday, in shelters and on duty as well as at home, and donated to the foundation's drone-based air-defence project as they did. A local outlet reported about UAH 1 million raised by midday and said the collection was still open. No final total has been published yet. ([Tvoe Misto](https://tvoemisto.tv/uk/news/12827-lyst-yakyy-ne-zakinchuyetsya-yak-ukrayina-ta-svit-razom-pysaly-vseukrayinskyy-radiodyktant-natsionalnoyi-yednosti-i-zibraly-ponad-milyon-hryven), [UNN](https://unn.ua/en/news/in-trenches-on-duty-and-in-shelters-how-ukraine-held-the-national-unity-radio-dictation))
- **[Defense tech] Ukrainian company Himera and Denmark's GomSpace are operating a test satellite for a sovereign Ukrainian communications network.** The satellite, OBRIA NANO, reached orbit on October 1. The companies say it gives them hands-on experience for a nationwide network under Ukrainian control that could later work alongside European systems. The news lands as Ukraine looks for alternatives to Starlink. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/himera-obria-nano-satellite/))
- **[Drones] Brave1 says two cheap interceptors against Russia's jet-powered Shaheds are nearly ready for combat.** Brave1, the government's defence-tech cluster, says 22 Ukrainian companies are developing interceptor drones, 12 of them with state funding. It is aiming for about $20,000–25,000 per launch. That compares with an estimated $100,000–150,000 for each jet-powered Shahed. The interceptor makers were not named. ([The Defense Post](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/10/27/ukraine-interceptors-russias-drones/))
- **[Defense tech] Nova Poshta says its own private air-defence team has shot down Russian Shahed drones.** Co-owner Vyacheslav Klymov said Ukraine's largest private delivery company formed the force to protect its facilities. He gave no numbers, and the claim has not been independently verified. ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukrainian-post-service-forms-private-air-defense-force-shoots-down-russian-drones/))
- **[Drones/AI] TAF Industries adds DoD Solution's AI autonomy module to its best-selling FPV drone.** The two Ukrainian companies say they chose the Kolibri 10 Advance because it is TAF's highest-volume model, so the module reaches more troops. They say the combined drone is already on Brave1 Market and has been used in combat by two brigades. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/taf-industries-dod-solution-partnership/))
- **[Drones] Ukrainian maker PUF releases Ban-One, an interceptor drone against Shaheds and small drones, with prices listed on Brave1 Market.** Units can order the drone alone or as a full system with a ground control station. The full system costs much more. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/news/kapitan-banan-1793089575))
- **[Drones/AI] SkyFall's StarGate drone evacuated two wounded soldiers within a week.** A Ukrainian assault unit used the drone after ground vehicles could not reach the soldiers. The unit says the drone's AI-assisted navigation kept it flying under Russian jamming. These are the unit's own claims. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/code-9-2-stargate-evac/))
- **[Drones] Export delays and US visa waits slow Ukrainian firms in the Pentagon's drone contest.** The Pentagon moved the next phase of its Drone Dominance Program from October to November, and at least one Ukrainian company withdrew. Other Ukrainian firms, including General Cherry and F-Drones, are setting up production in the US. ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/export-hurdles-and-visa-waits-for-ukrainian-companies-force-pentagon-to-slow-its-drone-contest/))

## Worth watching

- **Dronopad final total:** the Radio Dictation collection is still open. Watch for Come Back Alive or Suspilne to publish the final figure.
- **Interceptor race and sovereign comms:** Brave1's jet-Shahed interceptors are close to combat use, and private satellite tests like OBRIA NANO are under way. Both point to Ukrainian makers trying to rely less on foreign systems such as Starlink.
