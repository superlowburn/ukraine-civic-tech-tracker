# Ukraine Civic Tech — Wednesday, November 4, 2026

Covers items published from Tuesday morning, November 3, to Wednesday morning, November 4, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[Civilian apps / Cyber] The "Air Alert" team gives its first explanation of the weekend hack.** The volunteer-run team behind the app says attackers got unauthorized access to the operator interface that civil-protection operators use to send alerts. They did not get into the consumer app itself. That access let them push false warnings and Russian-language messages linking to an outside Telegram channel. The team says a security audit is now underway. It is withholding some details for security reasons and again denies that any user data leaked. ([Radio Svoboda](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/news-komanda-povitriana-tryvoha-nepravdyvi-spovishchennia/33889939.html))
- **[Defense tech] The EU and France's Bpifrance launch a €150 million guarantee programme to pair French firms with Ukrainian defense startups.** The programme covers drones, communications, navigation, space and critical components. Ukrainian companies can borrow to buy from EU suppliers. Reuters-cited targets put the goal at about 30 French–Ukrainian partnerships over three years. ([DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/11/03/eu-bpifrance-150m-french-ukrainian-defense-partnerships/))
- **[Defense tech] Norway sets aside about €86 million over three years to draw private Norwegian capital into Ukraine's defense industry.** State investor Investinor will back Norway-based funds that invest in Ukrainian defense companies, rather than funding firms directly. ([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/norway-to-invest-86-million-in-ukraine-s-defense-industry/))
- **[Drones] Vyriy Industries exits the Blyskavka strike-drone project after helping bring it to serial production.** The drone was developed by engineers, veterans and special-forces personnel. Its team will now run development and sales on its own, with a new financial partner. Vyriy says it is refocusing on its own products and R&D and did not disclose the deal's terms. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/vyriy-industries-zavershyla-uchast-u-proiekti-blyskavka-ta-vykhodyt-iz-partnerstva-1793712149), [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/vyriy-industries-mass-production-blyskavka/))
- **[Drones / AI] Brave1 supervisory-board chair Oleksandr Bornyakov predicts AI will soon make some targeting decisions without operator commands, starting in "kill zones."** He also says he is working with Ukrainian developers on networks of autonomous drones. He sees humanoid combat robots as a long-term idea, not a near-term one. These are his views in an interview, not a new programme. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/killzone-shi-1793718450))
- **[Drones] Ukrainian company Vitrolom unveils Krit, a light, low-cost, single-use ground robot built at the military's request.** It says it has started selling to Defense Forces units. It has not disclosed production numbers, confirmed deliveries or combat results. ([UA.NEWS](https://ua.news/en/ukraine/ukrayinska-kompaniia-predstavila-nadlegkii-nazemnii-robot-krit))
- **[Sanctions] KSE Institute and the B4Ukraine coalition warn that Russia is seizing European companies' local operations to pressure the EU over sanctions.** They urge EU and G7 governments to push for fast, responsible exits and greater financial transparency. ([KSE](https://kse.ua/en/news/russia-expands-seizures-of-european-business-assets-to-pressure-eu-governments-over-sanctions-joint-statement-by-kse-institute-and-b4ukraine/))
- **[OSINT] Molfar Intelligence's Minos service publishes its weekly open-source tally of Russian strikes on Ukrainian settlements.** It counted 219 settlements hit from October 26 to November 1, about 11% fewer than the week before. Strikes rose 32% in the Kharkiv, Dnipropetrovsk and Sumy regions, and housing was the most often damaged category. ([AIN](https://ain.ua/2026/11/03/rf-castise-atakuje-sxid-i-pivnicnii-sxid-ukrayini-shho-pokazav-analiz-minos/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on several fronts:

- IT Army or other hacktivist claims.
- InformNapalm or DeepState publications.
- New campaigns from the major foundations (Come Back Alive, Prytula, Sternenko, UNITED24).
- Diia defense-related launches.

- **Brave1 grant deadline is tomorrow:** Applications for the radar, air-to-air missile and turbojet engine grants close November 5.
- **Air Alert audit:** Watch for its results, and for whether other civilian-built alert tools tighten their operator-side security.
