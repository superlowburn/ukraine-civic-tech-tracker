# Ukraine Civic Tech — Saturday, November 7, 2026

Covers items published from Friday morning, November 6, to Saturday morning, November 7, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[Crowdfunding] Sternenko's foundation signs a cooperation memorandum with Ukraine's anti-corruption bureau (NABU) and prosecutor's office (SAPO).** The deal covers sharing information on defense pricing and procurement. It also sets up public reporting on military purchases paid for with money from plea deals and bail. SAPO will publish those reports on its website. Euromaidan Press reports the fund has already received about $1.25 million through such agreements. It also reports the fund has bought nearly 300,000 FPV drones overall; that count is the fund's own. ([Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/11/06/ukraines-anti-corruption-agencies-team-up-with-top-volunteer-whose-fund-bought-299365-drones-1-25-million-from-their-previous-deals-goes-to-army/))
- **[Drones] Ukraine's ground robots flew a record 31,398 missions in October, the Defense Ministry says.** That is more than four times January's count. Most were logistics and casualty-evacuation runs. Each logged mission earns the unit e-points, which it can spend on equipment from the Brave1 Market. This ties the robot makers' sales directly to frontline use. ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraines-ground-drones-complete-over-30-000-missions-in-record-month-for-robot-warfare/))
- **[AI / Demining] AI demining startup UADamage raises money from US investors Pravo Ventures and Ondas Capital.** UADamage was founded in 2022. Its platform reads satellite, drone and sensor data to find minefields and grade building damage for rebuilding. The money will go into the platform and into rolling it out with demining groups abroad. The amount was not disclosed. ([Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/89368))
- **[Crowdfunding] Ukrainian businesses are cutting donations because of Russian strikes, says the CEO of the Serhiy Prytula Charity Foundation.** In an AIN interview, Andriy Shuvalov said:
  - companies now provide about 70% of the fund's income;
  - some long-time corporate donors have stopped after their sites were hit;
  - the average donation is now about a third of its 2022 level;
  - the fund is looking for foreign donors and grants;
  - it is developing a Ukrainian-made data link for drones to cut reliance on imported satellite links, with a presentation planned around the New Year.

  ([AIN](https://ain.ua/2026/11/06/70-nadxodzen-vid-biznesu-fond-sergiia/))
- **[Drones] UGV Laboratory shows an updated line of its Hyena ground robots.** The logistics version is already in serial production and in use with Ukraine's Defense Forces. The upgraded flagship is in final combat testing before mass production. A strike version is still a prototype. These are the developer's own statements. ([LB.ua](https://lb.ua/tech/2026/11/06/779064_ugv_laboratory_predstavila.html))
- **[Defense tech] At an LB.Talks panel, Victory Drones head Mariia Berlinska says Ukraine is not winning the technology race.** She called for engineers with access to intelligence who would forecast threats six to twelve months ahead. The panel also included:
  - Deputy Defense Minister Ganna Hvozdyar, who said long-term contracts are not yet realistic;
  - Gunsmiths Council chair Maksym Vasylchenko, who asked for clear rules and joint ventures with foreign partners;
  - F-Drones CEO Stanislav Khutor, who said other countries are copying the Brave1 Market.

  ([LB.ua](https://lb.ua/society/2026/11/06/779104_chim_voyuvati_zavtra_reportazh.html))
- **[Defense tech] AIN asks whether defense tech is a bubble, and Ukrainian investors disagree.** The piece contrasts companies that have contracts with ones valued far above their sales. One example: Swarmer, the Nasdaq-listed Ukrainian drone company, has a market value of more than 300 times its 2025 revenue. Resist.UA's Roman Sulzhyk sees a bubble forming that may not do harm. Double Tap Investments sees overheating only in some segments. ([AIN](https://ain.ua/2026/11/06/miliardi-na-zbroiu-maibutnyogo-ci-formujetsia-investiciina-bulbaska-v-defense-tech/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on several fronts:

- InformNapalm, DeepState or KSE Institute publications.

## Worth watching

- **Pressure on the money behind civilian defense:** Prytula's fund reports shrinking business donations, and Sternenko's fund now has an extra funding route through anti-corruption recoveries. Watch whether other foundations sign similar deals with NABU and SAPO, or report the same drop in corporate giving.
- **Air Alert and Kyiv Independent hacks:** Still no published audit or formal attribution.
