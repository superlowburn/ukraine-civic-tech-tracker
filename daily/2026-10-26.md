# Ukraine Civic Tech — Monday, October 26, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Saturday morning, October 24, to Monday morning, October 26, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in the October 24 and 25 briefs are left out. It was a quiet weekend, so this brief is short.

## Top items

- **[Drones] F-Drones is building a cheap jet-powered interceptor against Russia's jet Shaheds.** The Ukrainian company is developing the F-Jet 80 and aims for a price of about $5,000–7,000 so it can be mass-produced. A company engineer says it has already destroyed several targets. That is the company's own claim and has not been independently verified. The drone is still in development. ([Militarnyi, citing The New York Times](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/ukrainian-company-f-drones-develops-low-cost-f-jet-80-interceptor-to-counter-shaheds/))
- **[Drones] Ratel Robotics starts building interceptor drones that launch from its ground robots.** Founder and CEO Taras Ostapchuk told Forbes Ukraine that the company also wants shared software so its ground, air and later sea drones can coordinate. He said Ratel has spent about $200,000 on the air side so far, and that fixed-wing models would cost several million dollars more. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/perekhopliuvachi-z-nrk-1793005126))
- **[Civilian AI] ML engineers build AI tools for documenting war crimes at AI HOUSE Camp.** 28 engineers in 7 teams worked on two problems. The first was tools that recognise Russian unit insignia in photos and videos for the War Archive project, to help link occupying units to specific war crimes. The second was a tool that strips personal data from Ukrainian texts such as court decisions. Participants also raised over UAH 70,000 for the volunteer group Reaktyvna Poshta. The camp ran October 6–11 and the results were published October 26. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/ai-house-camp-2026-1792994466))
- **[Civilian apps] A free navigator that works without GPS.** Ukrainian developers released LOST, an iPhone app that estimates a car's position from speed and travel time instead of satellite signals. They built it after GPS kept failing in cities during air attacks. It works offline on routes mapped in advance. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/lost-1792939084))
- **[Hardware] BlueBird Tech launches a repair service for Starlink terminals damaged in the field.** The Ukrainian company says there is no official Starlink service centre in Ukraine. Its service diagnoses and fixes faults such as water ingress, overheating, power failures and damaged housings, for military and other users. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/bluebird-tech-zapustyla-servis-iz-remontu-starlink-1793007820))
- **[Drones] Musk says SpaceX "simply enforced our terms" as full Starlink speed returns.** After days of blaming Washington, Musk said SpaceX restored full speed on Saturday. He repeated that the limits closed a gap Ukrainian forces used for drone strikes. Volunteers and advisers had disputed that claim (covered October 25), and SpaceX has given no details. ([Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/10/25/full-speed-returned-to-ukraines-satellite-links-on-saturday-morning-we-simply-enforced-our-terms-musk-claims-after-blaming-washington-for-10-days/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on volunteer cyber or OSINT groups (IT Army, Ukrainian Cyber Alliance, InformNapalm, DeepState) or KSE Institute's sanctions trackers (their latest is still dated October 7). The same goes for Diia, and for new campaigns from the major foundations (Come Back Alive, Prytula, Sternenko, UNITED24). UNITED24's newest posted update is dated October 17.
