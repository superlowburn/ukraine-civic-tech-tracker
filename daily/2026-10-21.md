# Ukraine Civic Tech — Wednesday, October 21, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Tuesday morning, October 20, to Wednesday morning, October 21, 2026 (Kyiv time).

## Top items

- **[Drones] General Cherry says it can't sign drone contracts it won in a state tender.** The drone maker, which began as a volunteer group, told Defender Media it can't sign contracts for four lots totalling 31,000 drones. It says there is no document setting out the procedure or delivery deadlines, and it wants the tender cancelled and rerun more transparently. The Defence Procurement Agency (DPA) replied that the courts have upheld the procedure and that contracts are still being concluded. The Sternenko Community Foundation has warned that moving such tenders onto the open Prozorro system could expose weapon specifications and production sites. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/general-cherry-dpa-tender-issues/))
- **[Crowdfunding] Lithuanian volunteers raise €1.3M for anti-drone defence.** The charity Blue/Yellow's eight-week "Dronaton" raised about €1.3M for interceptor drones, anti-drone systems and operator training. 80% goes to Ukraine's military and 20% to the Lithuanian Riflemen's Union. ([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/1-3-million-anti-drone-defense-ukr-lith/))
- **[Drones] TENCORE's German joint venture aims to build its first ground robots by year-end.** Quantum Tencore Industries, TENCORE's joint venture with Germany's Quantum Systems under the "Build with Ukraine" programme, plans to start producing TerMIT ground robots in Germany by the end of 2026. CEO Maksym Vasylchenko said some permits are still pending. He blamed the delay partly on staff shortages at Ukraine's export-control service. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/quantum-tencore-start-production-by-2006-end/))
- **[Drones] DevDroid signs a production agreement with a Norwegian electronics maker.** The Ukrainian ground-robot developer and Inission Norge AS signed a memorandum to jointly produce remote-control systems for Ukraine's Defence Forces, and later possibly for Scandinavian buyers. They haven't disclosed volumes or timelines. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/devdroid-inission-norge/), [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/devdroid-inission-norge-joint-manufacture/), [Forbes Ukraine](https://forbes.ua/news/ukrayinska-devdroid-zalucaje-norvezkogo-partnera-do-virobnictva-sistem-keruvannia-nazemnimi-robotami-20102026-43130))
- **[Drones] ANT upgrades its Mercury reconnaissance drone.** The Ukrainian company says the new version flies longer and copes better with electronic jamming. These are the company's own claims. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/mercury-upgraded/), [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/upgraded-ukrainian-mercury-uas-gains-extended-range-and-improved-ew-resistance/))
- **[Drones] AIN.UA opens its 2026 defence-tech awards.** The Ukrainian tech outlet is taking nominations for defence-tech startups, growth-stage companies and investors until November 9. One special award covers robotic evacuation and life-protection technology. ([AIN.ua](https://ain.ua/2026/10/20/ain-awards-2026/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on volunteer cyber/OSINT groups, Ukrainian civilian alert or AI apps, or the major Ukrainian foundations (Come Back Alive, Prytula, Sternenko, UNITED24). Diia's new services that day, such as a test of online construction notices and child-benefit applications, are civic but not defence-related.

## Worth watching

- **Openness vs. secrecy in drone procurement:** the General Cherry–DPA dispute sets calls for open Prozorro tenders against warnings from volunteer foundations that this would leak sensitive data. Watch whether the agency changes its rules or contract awards slip.
- **"Build with Ukraine" bottlenecks:** export-control permits now appear to be slowing joint production abroad, as in the TENCORE/Quantum and DevDroid/Inission deals. This comes as Brave1 searches for a new CEO.
