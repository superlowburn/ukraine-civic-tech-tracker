# Ukraine Civic Tech — Tuesday, November 3, 2026

Covers items published from Monday morning, November 2, to Tuesday morning, November 3, 2026 (Kyiv time), plus one later Tuesday follow-up on Monday's lead story. Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[Civilian apps / Cyber] The "Air Alert" app is fully back, and Zelensky says Ukraine hacked Russia's alert app in response.** The app's team said service was restored within about two hours. Ajax Systems and developer Stfalcon had switched off push notifications and paused the app. The team said the app stores no personal data or locations, so users' phones and accounts are not at risk, and it is strengthening the app's security. In a PBS interview reported Tuesday, Zelensky said Ukraine hit back at Russia's emergency alert app after the November 1 attack, saying "We only respond to their attacks." He did not say which body carried out the hack. ATESH's earlier claim that its hackers did it remains unverified. ([Glavcom](https://glavcom.ua/techno/telecom/robotu-zastosunku-povitrjana-trivoha-povnistju-vidnovleno-chi-stavsja-vitik-danikh-1152994.html), [BankInfoSecurity](https://www.bankinfosecurity.com/ukraine-russia-early-warning-systems-hacked-a-33271), [Ukrinform](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/4180868-ukraine-hacked-russias-emergency-alert-system-in-response-to-russian-cyberattack-zelensky.html))
- **[Cyber] Belarusian Cyber Partisans and the pro-Ukrainian group Silent Crow claim a destructive hack of Rosstat, Russia's statistics agency.** The groups say they spent months inside its network and took records including mortality and military casualty data. Rosstat confirmed a cyberattack but denies that any data leaked or that its databases were compromised. The Record notes that the scale of the intrusion, the damage and the data theft are **self-reported and unverified**. The groups say they will release more details and data samples later. ([The Record](https://therecord.media/hackers-ukraine-belarus-russia))
- **[Drones / Defense tech] Brave1 launches Brave1 Invest Portal to match Ukrainian defence-tech makers with investors.** Investors see the catalogue only after compliance checks, which include screening for ties to Russia and its allies. Producers create profiles, moderated by Brave1, that list their funding needs and stage. Sensitive technical details stay out of the profiles, and pitch decks are shared only on request. ([LB.ua](https://lb.ua/society/2026/11/03/778148_brave1_zapuskaie_investitsiynu.html))
- **[Defense tech] Ukraine's Defence Ministry signs a cooperation memorandum with NATO's innovation accelerator DIANA.** The ministry calls it the first agreement of its kind between DIANA and a NATO partner country. Plans include a catalogue of Ukrainian defence technology that lines up with NATO countries' catalogues, joint projects, and possible testing in Ukraine. NATO says Ukrainian developers will find it easier to understand NATO requirements and to scale. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/11/ukraine-nato-defence-sooperation/))
- **[Drones] Kyiv startup CODE380's pocket-sized receiver lets Ukrainian units watch the live video feeds of many Russian drones.** TechCrunch reports that the device can show where an incoming drone is heading before it strikes. It notes that Russia's shift to digital video links could eventually blind receivers of this kind. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/ukraines-new-pocket-sized-gadget-turns-russian-drones-cameras-against-them/))
- **[Hardware] BlueBird Tech unveils the Chuyka 3 Plus, an upgraded portable detector that warns soldiers of approaching FPV drones.** It is a new version of the Ukrainian company's 2025 detector. The announcement gave no price or sales channel. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/11/bluebird-chuyka-3-plus/))
- **[Drones] SkyFall's StarGate drone carries out a third casualty evacuation in about a week and a half.** The CODE 9.2 unit of the 475th Assault Regiment used it to rescue a wounded soldier from a neighbouring unit. He had been stuck at his position for 57 days, according to the developer. This follows the first evacuations covered last week. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/11/3-stargate-evacuation/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on several fronts:

- IT Army claims.
- New InformNapalm, DeepState or KSE Institute publications.
- New campaigns from the major foundations (Come Back Alive, Prytula, Sternenko, UNITED24).
- New Diia launches. The Digital Ministry only began its annual Digital Literacy Month and published an overview of dual-use tech.

## Worth watching

- **Alert apps as a cyber front:** Ukraine has now openly said it retaliated, so watch for further tit-for-tat attacks on civilian warning systems. Also watch for a post-mortem from Ajax, Stfalcon or CERT-UA on how the Air Alert app was breached.
- **Brave1 grant deadline:** Applications for the radar, air-to-air missile and turbojet engine grants close November 5.
