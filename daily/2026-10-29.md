# Ukraine Civic Tech — Thursday, October 29, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Wednesday morning, October 28, to Thursday morning, October 29, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[Counter-disinformation] Euromaidan Press gets a Russian clone of its site taken down.** The volunteer-founded Ukrainian outlet says a look-alike domain copied its whole site and added a fake story blaming a Ukrainian drone for Russia's October 8 bombing of buses in Kramatorsk. An AI-made video spread alongside it. The outlet filed abuse and copyright complaints with the hosting and security providers, and the clone is now offline. The Gnida Project and NewsGuard both link the operation to Storm-1516, a Russian influence campaign that impersonates trusted news brands. ([Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/10/29/a-russian-disinformation-campaign-cloned-euromaidan-press-to-blame-ukraine-for-russias-own-strike-on-kramatorsk-we-took-the-fake-down/))
- **[Drones] Brave1 Market adds a category for amphibious ground robots.** The Defence Ministry says units can now see the first three amphibious UGVs on Brave1's online marketplace. These robots carry supplies across rivers, canals and marshland, and units will later be able to order them through the ePoints programme. Brave1 opened a grant track for amphibious UGVs in June. The makers were not named. ([Ministry of Defence](https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/ukraine-amphibious-ugvs-available-on-brave1-market), [LB.ua](https://lb.ua/society/2026/10/28/776830_brave1_market_dodali.html))
- **[Drones/AI] NPR profiles The Fourth Law, one of Ukraine's leading makers of AI-assisted drones.** Founder Yaroslav Azhnyuk, who started the pet-camera company Petcube before the war, says dozens of military units use the company's autonomous drone. He says humans still choose targets. NPR reports the company's claims about the drone's effectiveness without independent checks. ([NPR via WVXU](https://www.wvxu.org/news-from-npr/2026-10-29/how-ai-drones-are-influencing-the-course-of-the-ukraine-war))
- **[Drones] The Defence Ministry says Ukraine is first in the world to develop drones for evacuating the wounded.** The ministry says a heavy hexacopter from a Brave1 participant carried out a medical evacuation in October. It frames this, together with the StarGate drone covered on Wednesday, as a new Brave1-led class of technology. The ministry gave no funding figures. ([LB.ua](https://lb.ua/tech/2026/10/28/776780_minoboroni_ukrayina_pershoyu.html))
- **[Defense tech] Nordic funds are putting money into Ukrainian defence startups.** Defender Media profiled three investors. Norway's Gardar has raised over €80M and invested about €7M, including in Buntar Aerospace and M-Fly. Denmark's Final Frontier is raising a €100M second fund. Sweden's Varangians has backed Himera and others. The investors named corruption concerns, weak shareholder protections and slow bank transfers as obstacles. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/what-motivates-nordic-investors/))
- **[Civilian apps] The Digital Ministry is preparing "Diia.Office" for civil-service hiring.** Through the service, people will be able to browse government job openings in the Diia app, upload or build a CV, and apply. The ministry gave no launch date. ([Hromadske Radio](https://hromadske.radio/news/2026/10/28/mintsyfry-zapuskaie-diia-ofis))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on several fronts:

- IT Army or other Ukrainian hacker groups' claims.
- New InformNapalm or DeepState investigations; DeepState kept up routine map updates.
- KSE Institute's sanctions and shadow-fleet trackers. KSE's latest is still the September tracker, published October 8.
- New campaigns from Prytula, Sternenko or UNITED24.
- The final Dronopad total, which neither Come Back Alive nor Suspilne has published yet.

## Worth watching

- **Clone-site attacks on Ukrainian media:** Euromaidan Press is the latest Ukrainian outlet to be impersonated in a campaign that now uses AI-made video. Watch for other outlets reporting clones and for how quickly hosting companies act.
- **Money and marketplace growth:** Brave1 Market keeps adding new product categories and foreign funds are raising money for Ukrainian defence startups. Together, these show civilian defence-tech growing into a funded industry.
