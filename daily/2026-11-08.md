# Ukraine Civic Tech — Sunday, November 8, 2026

Covers items published from Saturday morning, November 7, to Sunday morning, November 8, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out. It was a quiet weekend window, so this brief is short.

## Top items

- **[Cyber] The hackers behind the Rosstat breach publish their own estimates of Russia's war dead, based on the records they say they took.** Belarusian Cyber Partisans count at least 393,881 deaths. They include only death records that name military operations as the cause. The pro-Ukrainian group Silent Crow uses a broader method and reaches up to 450,100. It publishes its figures on a dedicated leak site. Both counts cover February 2022 to late August 2026. The Kyiv Independent could not verify the records or the estimates. Rosstat still denies that any data leaked. All figures are the **hackers' own, unverified claims**. ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/hackers-claim-leaked-russian-data-reveals-up-to-450-000-war-related-deaths-in-ukraine/), [Reform.news](https://reform.news/en/cyber-partisans-calculate-russia-s-losses-based-on-hacked-rosstat-database/))
- **[OSINT] InformNapalm counts 242 sabotage incidents inside Russia so far in 2026, a record.** That is more than in 2024 and 2025 combined, and about 88% above the 2025 total. The volunteer OSINT community logs publicly reported incidents in a database that now holds 569 cases since February 2022. The database leaves out occupied Ukrainian territory and Ukrainian drone and missile strikes. It relies on open-source reports only. InformNapalm says its data does not show that Ukrainian intelligence organized every incident. ([UNITED24 Media](https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/sabotage-attacks-inside-russia-surge-88-as-moscow-expands-covert-operations-across-europe-24171), [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/russia-records-more-sabotage-incidents-in-2026-than-in-previous-two-years-combined/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on several fronts:

- Volunteer drone makers and defense-tech startups.
- Brave1 announcements.
- IT Army claims.
- Diia or Digital Ministry launches.
- New campaigns from the major foundations (Come Back Alive, Prytula, Sternenko, UNITED24).

## Worth watching

- **Rosstat leak:** The two groups disagree on method, and the Cyber Partisans plan a separate count of Belarusians killed fighting for Russia. Watch for independent analysis of the leaked records, for example by Mediazona or BBC Russian, which have their own open-source death count.
- **Air Alert and Kyiv Independent hacks:** Still no published audit or formal attribution.
