# Ukraine Civic Tech — Saturday, October 24, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Friday morning, October 23, to Saturday morning, October 24, 2026 (Kyiv time). There was no brief on October 23, so a few items announced late on October 22 and first reported on October 23 are included.

## Top items

- **[Drones] Sine Engineering opens an R&D centre in Finland.** The Ukrainian company makes communications and navigation systems that it says more than 200 Ukrainian drone makers use. It announced a joint Ukrainian–Finnish R&D centre for resilient communications, navigation and onboard autonomy, and plans to grow its Finnish team to 100 people by the end of 2026. It also signed a memorandum with Finnish tech firm Insta, without giving details. Sine hopes the project will be included in the Ukraine–Finland "Drone Deal" so that technology-transfer permits come faster. ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraines-sine-engineering-launches-joint-defense-tech-initiative-with-finland/), [Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/sine-group-finland/))
- **[Drones] A Ukrainian interceptor-drone maker teams up with Sweden's Scandinavian X to bid for NATO contracts.** Under a memorandum of intent, the Swedish joint venture would act as lead supplier, and the Ukrainian partner would provide the products and the expertise. Their first joint bid is planned for November 2026. Defender Media describes the Ukrainian company only as one of the country's largest drone makers, and no financial terms were disclosed. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/scandinavian-x-ukrainian-interceptors/))
- **[Drones] Ukrainian defence firms can now bid in NATO tenders.** Ukraine has become a partner of the NATO Support and Procurement Agency (NSPA). Ukrainian manufacturers can register in its supplier database, take part in NATO tenders and sign contracts with the agency. Ukraine's Defence Ministry says Ukraine is the first non-NATO country with this kind of single-point partnership. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/ukrainian-manufacturers-nato-tenders/))
- **[Drones] The government settles staff draft deferrals for defence companies: below 100%, with a path back to full deferral.** Prime Minister Serhii Koretskyi said defence companies keep their critical status. However, the maximum share of staff they can defer from the draft will fall below 100%, and no exact figure has been given. A route to 100% deferral for companies that meet their mobilisation obligations is being drafted for approval by December 1. Companies that need a higher share can also apply case by case. This settles the dispute covered on October 22. Industry reaction wasn't reported yet. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/government-resolution-on-deferrals/))
- **[Drones] Volunteers disagree on whether the Starlink limits are over.** Volunteer and foundation head Serhiy Sternenko told dev.ua he thinks Starshield access has been restored, mainly because of pressure in the US. He said Starlink can't realistically be fully replaced soon. Instead, he called for replacing it piece by piece with wired and other non-satellite links, with the state creating demand for these options. Defence-tech adviser Serhii "Flash" Beskrestnov said the same day that he was seeing no lifting of the speed limits. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/my-proponuvaly-rishennia-iake-mohlo-chastkovo-zaminyty-starlink-sternenko-rozpoviv-pro-ponovlennia-roboty-starshield-i-chastkovi-alternatyvy-tsiiei-tekhnolohii-dlia-frontu-1792735715), [Censor.net](https://censor.net/en/news/4029987/the-restrictions-on-starlink-have-not-yet-been-completely-lifted))
- **[Cyber] The Prime Minister tells state agencies to set up cybersecurity teams.** Koretskyi said Russia struck 10 data centres in Kyiv last month and that AI-assisted cyberattacks on state systems and businesses are rising. He said Russia is attacking digital infrastructure as systematically as it attacks the energy sector. ([Reuters via ThePrint](https://theprint.in/world/russia-steps-up-attacks-on-ukraines-digital-infrastructure-kyiv-says/3080011/))
- **[Drones] F-Drones' F-Captain strike drone is now on Brave1 Market and DOT-Chain Defence.** Units can order it through both marketplaces and pay with ePoints on Brave1 Market. The Ukrainian maker says it built the drone in response to a military request for an affordable mid-range system. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/f-captain-brave-1-market-dot-chain/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on volunteer cyber or OSINT groups such as the IT Army, Ukrainian Cyber Alliance or InformNapalm. The same goes for KSE Institute's sanctions trackers (their latest publication is dated October 7), civilian alert or AI apps, Diia, and the major foundations (Come Back Alive, Prytula, Sternenko, UNITED24).

## Worth watching

- **Moving off Starlink:** Ukraine's Defence Ministry signed a letter of intent with Finland's ICEYE and Nokia on its own satellite-communications system ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/23/ukraine-plans-independent-satellite-system-as-starlink-issues-persist)). Volunteers are pushing for non-satellite links in the meantime. Watch whether foundations or Brave1 start funding these.
- **Exports go through Northern Europe:** Sine's Finnish R&D centre, the Scandinavian X deal and NSPA access all route Ukrainian defence tech through partners in the north. The open questions are export permits and whether the deferral rules leave companies enough engineers.
