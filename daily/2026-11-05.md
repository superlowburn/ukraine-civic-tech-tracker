# Ukraine Civic Tech — Thursday, November 5, 2026

Covers items published from Wednesday morning, November 4, to Thursday morning, November 5, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[Defense tech] Ukraine widens its private air-defence experiment for companies.** The Defence Ministry announced six changes on November 4. Companies taking part can now:
  - protect other businesses' sites under contract;
  - pass equipment to one another, with Air Force approval;
  - use manned aircraft and armed interceptor drones;
  - take on team members from age 18 rather than 21.

  Defense City residents and firms the ministry rates as critical can now join too. All teams stay under military command. This builds on the corporate teams covered last week, such as Nova Poshta's. ([UNITED24 Media](https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/ukraine-expands-private-air-defense-to-manned-aircraft-and-warhead-equipped-interceptor-drones-24102))
- **[Cyber] The Kyiv Independent is hit by a cyberattack that sent fake "news" to its readers.** On November 4, intruders got into the outlet's content system and one of its newsletter services. They sent four emails and posted website messages in Russian and English with false claims about President Zelensky. Staff deleted them and are working with security specialists. The outlet says payment details are handled by third parties and are not at risk. It suspects a Russian operation, though this is its own assessment and no one has formally attributed the attack. It notes similarities to the Air Alert app hack three days earlier. ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/kyiv-independent-hit-by-suspected-russian-cyberattack/))
- **[AI / Demining] Demining startup UADamage raises money from Ondas Capital and Pravo Ventures.** Ondas Capital is the investment arm of the US firm Ondas. Pravo Ventures is a US syndicate focused on Ukraine. The round size was not disclosed. UADamage uses AI and geospatial data to spot explosive hazards and map risk for humanitarian deminers. It says it will use the money to scale up, expand abroad and strengthen training and field support. ([Interfax-Ukraine](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/1231361.html), [Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/11/uadamage-investment-pravo-ondas/))
- **[Civilian apps] The European Commission raises its score for Ukraine's digital readiness for EU membership.** The 2026 Enlargement Report gives Ukraine's digital and media chapter 4 out of 5, up from 3.5, according to the Digital Ministry. The ministry says the report cites the Diia ecosystem, the Diia.AI assistant and closer work with EU cybersecurity bodies. ([Government of Ukraine / Digital Ministry](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/news/mintsyfry-nablyzhaiemosia-do-tsyfrovoho-prostoru-ies-ievrokomisiia-pidvyshchyla-otsinku-hotovnosti-ukrainy))
- **[Drones] Militech Group offers the "Grim" modem as a backup way to control drones and ground robots when Starlink is limited.** The Ukrainian company says it built the modem after October's Starlink disruptions. It says the modem is tested, ready for combat use and available through Brave1, and that it plans foreign sales from January 2027. These are the company's own claims. ([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/ukraine-develops-grim-lte-modem-to-help-control-drones-without-starlinks/))
- **[Drones] Ukrainian company IRV shows a prototype jet-powered interceptor against jet Shaheds.** It was shown at an industry exhibition, and the company claims a top speed of up to 750 km/h. IRV says it needs two to three months more work on the airframe, with serial production about three months after that. IRV is one of a growing number of jet-interceptor projects. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/ukrainska-kompaniia-planuie-zapustyty-v-seriiu-perekhopliuvachi-shcho-rozhaniatymutsia-do-750-kmhod-koly-tse-vidbudetsia-1793875044))
- **[Drones] Two more Ukrainian makers expand their drone lineups.**
  - ROBONEERS says its BULLDOG multi-purpose drone finished its first combat trials with a mechanized brigade. It plans to refine the design using the data.
  - Ratel Robotics announced a range of seven drone models, from strike FPVs to an interceptor. It did not say which models are in production.

  ([Militarnyi — ROBONEERS](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/roboneers-bulldog-drone-completes-first-combat-trials-with-the-23rd-brigade/), [Militarnyi — Ratel](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/ratel-robotics-develops-seven-uav-models-from-strike-drones-to-interceptors/))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on several fronts:

- IT Army or other hacktivist claims.
- InformNapalm, DeepState or KSE Institute publications.
- New campaigns from the major foundations (Come Back Alive, Prytula, Sternenko, UNITED24).

## Worth watching

- **Attacks on civilian information channels:** The Air Alert app and now the Kyiv Independent were both used to push fake messages within a week. On November 4, Ukraine's Center for Countering Disinformation said the Air Alert hack was meant to undermine trust in the warning system ([RBC-Ukraine](https://www.rbc.ua/rus/news/tspd-rozkriv-metu-rosiyskoyi-kiberataki-zastosunok-1793819386.html)). Watch for results from both outlets' security reviews and for any formal attribution.
- **Brave1 grants close today:** Applications for the radar, air-to-air missile and turbojet engine grants close November 5.
