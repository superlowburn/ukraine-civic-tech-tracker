# Ukraine Civic Tech — Tuesday, October 27, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Monday morning, October 26, to Tuesday morning, October 27, 2026 (Kyiv time). Items already covered in earlier briefs are left out.

## Top items

- **[OSINT] InformNapalm says Russian intelligence tried to stage a paid pro-Rosatom rally in Budapest.** The volunteer OSINT community and its partner Ukrainian Militant published an investigation alleging that officers linked to Russia's foreign intelligence service (SVR) tried to organise a rally outside Hungary's parliament in support of the Paks II nuclear plant, which Rosatom is building. The rally was cancelled shortly before it was due to start. The findings rest on leaked correspondence that a Ukrainian hacker group says it obtained. These are allegations; the leaked material has not been independently verified, and investigators found no proof that anyone was actually paid. ([UNITED24 Media](https://united24media.com/world/russian-spy-network-exposed-in-alleged-scheme-to-stage-fake-pro-nuclear-rally-in-budapest-23808), [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/russian-intelligence-services-tried-to-organize-rally-in-support-of-paks-ii-nuclear-plant-in-budapest-informnapalm/))
- **[Crowdfunding] The National Unity Radio Dictation raises money for Come Back Alive's "Dronopad."** Public broadcaster Suspilne ran the annual dictation on Ukrainian Writing and Language Day this morning, with a fundraiser for the foundation's drone-based air-defence project. No total had been published at the time of writing. ([PB News](https://pb-news.info/2026/10/27/vsi-detali-radiodyktantu-naczionalnoyi-yednosti-tekst-vid-voyina-artur-dronya-chytannya-u-dueti-ta-zbir-na-dronopad/), [Suspilne](https://suspilne.media/culture/1433745-pid-cas-radiodiktantu-suspilne-zbiratime-kosti-na-sistemu-ppo-dla-zbitta-rosijskih-droniv/))
- **[Drones] Wild Hornets' new drone video module passes state tests and goes into mass production.** The volunteer-founded maker says the module helps its FPV drones keep their link under Russian jamming and fly farther. It also says making the part in-house cut the cost of its drones for the army. These are the company's own claims about performance. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/novyi-modul-zviazku-vid-dykykh-shershniv-1793023147), [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/hv-vtx-pro-video-transmitter-wild-hornets/))
- **[Drones] Ukrainian company InLab's anti-drone detection and jamming systems are on Brave1 Market.** Defender Media profiled InLab's Boryslav detector and STRYI jamming station, which are meant to work together against Russian FPV drones. Units can order both through Brave1 Market, including with ePoints, and Boryslav has been codified for military use. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/boryslav-stryy-inlab/))
- **[Civilian apps] The Digital Ministry reports on getting connectivity and state data ready for winter.** In a three-month summary, the ministry said most mobile base stations now have battery or generator backup, and that it is expanding home internet that keeps working during power cuts. It said state registers have been moved to protected underground sites and cloud storage. It also reported 20+ new Diia services, a wider rollout of the Diia.AI assistant, and that the International Register of Damage now accepts claims in all 43 categories. ([Rayon.in.ua](https://life.rayon.in.ua/news/1111606-pidgotovka-zviazku-do-zimi-novi-poslugi-v-diyi-ta-rozvitok-dual-use-pidsumki-roboti-mincifri-za-tri-misiaci))
- **[Drones/Cyber] Ukrainian startups come back from TechCrunch Disrupt with investor interest.** The roundup includes defence maker CELEBRA Tech, which says it is in talks with an unnamed jet-engine manufacturer, and ThreatBreaker, an AI cyber-threat detection startup that says it is in talks with ABB about protecting industrial infrastructure. ([dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/techcrunch-disrupt-2026-u-san-frantsysko-1793027219))

I found no new, confirmed items from this window on IT Army or Ukrainian Cyber Alliance claims, DeepState, or KSE Institute's sanctions and shadow-fleet trackers. KSE's latest publication is still dated October 7. The same goes for new campaigns from Prytula, Sternenko or UNITED24.
