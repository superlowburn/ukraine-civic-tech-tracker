# Ukraine Civic Tech — Tuesday, October 20, 2026

Covers items published roughly from Monday morning, October 19, to Tuesday morning, October 20, 2026.

## Top items

- **[Drones] Brave1 CEO Andrii Hrytseniuk steps down.** The government-backed defence-tech cluster announced his departure after about 17 months. No successor has been named yet, and COO Iryna Zabolotna will steer the cluster during the transition. It follows the exit of investment chief Artem Moroz earlier this month, and no reason was given for either change. ([Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/more-change-at-brave1-andrii-hrytseniuk-departs-as-ceo-of-ukraines-lauded-defence-cluster), [Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/andrii-hrytseniuk-steppind-down-as-brave1-ceo/))
- **[Drones] Ukrainian startup's hexacopter used in a reported first airborne casualty evacuation.** Frontline units used a heavy-lift drone from Ukrainian maker Gurzuf Defence to evacuate a wounded soldier when ground robots couldn't get through. The brigade described it as a world first, and that claim hasn't been independently verified. The company's co-founder said air evacuation should stay a last resort because the risks to the wounded are too high. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/audag-6-evacuation/))
- **[Drones] TENCORE starts consolidating Ukraine's ground-robot makers.** The Ukrainian defence-tech company's CEO said it has bought or is buying equity stakes in two domestic robotics manufacturers, NODRICS and TOR, for its new partner ecosystem. Terms weren't disclosed, and he said further deals will need outside investment. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/tencore-kupuye/))
- **[Apps] Diia is now linked with the Kyiv Digital city app.** Veterans and people with disabilities can show their status for free parking with Diia documents instead of paper. The ministry also says the link will enable new services that need data shared between systems. ([Diia](https://diia.gov.ua/news/diia-kyiv-tsyfrovyi-sproshchuiemo-miski-servisy))
- **[Apps] More regions can now use Diia to apply for backup-power grants for apartment buildings.** The SvitloDim program pays housing associations up to UAH 300,000 per building for equipment that keeps heat, water and lifts running during blackouts. It has opened in four more regions as Russian strikes on the grid continue. ([Diia](https://diia.gov.ua/news/do-300-tys-hrn-na-enerhostiikist-budynku-dopomoha-dostupna-shche-u-4-oblastiakh))
- **[Drones] Ukrainian firms keep shipping new defence tools.** BlueBird Tech released an updated portable drone detector for military units, according to Militarnyi ([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/bluebird-tech-releases-updated-chuyka-4-5-0-drone-detector-with-expanded-frequency-range/)). ComBat Vision built a game-style tool to train new artillery recruits, saying many of them come from gaming backgrounds ([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/combat-vision-develops-gamified-training-tool-for-artillery-crews/)).
- **[Drones] Ukrainian-founded UFORCE's sea drones were tested by the US Army.** The company's uncrewed boats were used as launch platforms for aerial drones during a US Army exercise. This is a sign of Ukrainian battlefield tech moving into allied militaries. ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/us-army-magura-drone-bayonet/))

I found no new, confirmed items from the past 24 hours on volunteer cyber/OSINT groups or the major crowdfunding foundations. Earlier campaigns are still running, such as Kyivstar and the Sternenko Community Foundation's "Operation Interception", launched October 14.

## Worth watching

- **Brave1 leadership:** two senior departures in one month, coming after Mykhailo Fedorov left the Defence Ministry. Watch for a new CEO and whether the cluster's grant and marketplace priorities change.
- **Aerial casualty evacuation:** this could spread, but the maker itself warns it should stay an exception. Watch whether more units or charities start funding it.

## Update — Tuesday midday (Halifax time)

A second check of the same window found these items that the morning edition didn't cover:

- **[Cyber & OSINT] InformNapalm publishes #BabakovLeaks sanctions investigation.** The volunteer OSINT community says documents from the hacktivist group 256 Cyber Assault Division show how sanctioned State Duma deputy chairman Aleksandr Babakov's circle keeps working abroad through unsanctioned European intermediaries. It argues this is a blind spot in sanctions enforcement. The leaked documents and the findings are *self-reported and not independently verified*. ([InformNapalm](https://informnapalm.org/en/alexander-babakov-geoffroy-doly-sanctions), [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/russian-state-duma-s-deputy-chairman-aleksandr-babakov-bypasses-sanctions-with-help-from-french-citizen/))
- **[Drones] Ukrainian firm CODE380 unveils a portable drone-signal analyzer.** Militarnyi, citing DOU, reports that the handheld D·Vision SDR helps users spot nearby drones and view some of their video feeds. The company says it was field-tested with Ukrainian service members. ([Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/ukraine-d-vision-sdr-rf-analyzer-uav-video/))
- **[Drones] Most Ukrainian defence-tech money flows through foreign holding companies.** An AIN.ua analysis quotes industry figures who say almost all startups raise money through companies registered in the US, UK or Estonia, while R&D and production stay in Ukraine. One industry group expects $200–250M to come in this way in 2026. Supporters call it a necessary workaround, while critics worry Ukraine could end up as only a testing ground and talent pool. ([AIN.ua](https://ain.ua/2026/10/19/investiciyi-v-ukrayinskii-deftech-comu-milioni-dolariv-osidaiut-v-inozemnix-xoldingax-i-ci-je-v-cyomu-zagroza-dlia-ekonomiki/))

I found no new, confirmed crowdfunding or civilian-app items in the window beyond those above.

**Also worth watching:** Defender Media's October 20 follow-up describes TENCORE's ground-robot deals as completed purchases, and its CEO says more are coming ([Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/tencore-buys-tor-and-mat1lda/)). Together with the AIN analysis, this points to a sector starting to consolidate and look for outside capital.
