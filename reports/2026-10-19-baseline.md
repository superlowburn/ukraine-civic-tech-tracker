# Ukraine Civic Tech: Baseline to October 19, 2026

This is the starting point for the tracker. It summarises how Ukrainian civilians have used programming, AI and other technology to help defend Ukraine from February 2022 to October 19, 2026. "Civilians" here means volunteers, startups, civil society, hobbyists, the diaspora, and civic-facing government tech such as Diia. It does not cover the armed forces' own operations. From October 20, 2026, the daily briefs in [`daily/`](../daily/) add only new items.

**How to read this**
- Everything here is reported at news level. Figures from governments, companies, foundations or hacker groups are marked *self-reported* when no one has checked them independently.
- Hacktivist impact claims are the groups' own unless a source says otherwise.
- Dates are publication dates unless an event date is given.

---

## The big picture

- **Civilian drone-making grew into a state-backed industry.** In 2022 it was crowdfunded and volunteer-built. By 2026 there were about 500 drone companies, more than 1,000 Brave1 grants, and online marketplaces where front-line units order straight from manufacturers. The 2026 production target is about 10 million drones (*self-reported*).
- **Interceptor drones are now the main thing civilians fund.** Foundations, telecoms firms and diaspora campaigns are all paying for cheap interceptors against Shahed-type drones. That includes the jet-powered Shaheds now appearing.
- **Who runs the institutions changed sharply in 2026.**
  - Mykhailo Fedorov left the digital ministry to become defence minister in January and was dismissed in July.
  - Oksana Ferchuk became digital minister in July.
  - Brave1 lost both its investment chief and its CEO in October.
- **Ukraine is moving its public services onto AI.** Diia.AI went from a web beta (September 2025) to the main app with voice (2026), and the ministry is building a national language model.
- **Fewer people are giving, and big grants now drive the totals.** Small donations through monobank "jars" (online collection pots) and the share of households who give have both fallen since their 2024 peak. Large foreign-government grants now make up much of the major foundations' totals.
- **Cyber volunteers still have no legal status.** Volunteer hackers and OSINT groups keep claiming large operations against Russian targets. The bill that would give civilian "cyber reservists" a legal footing has stalled before its second reading.

## 1. Drones & defence tech

### Background (2022–2024)
- **Army of Drones (July 2022).** UNITED24, the General Staff and the digital ministry launched a crowdfunding programme for drones and pilot training. It raised $13.5M in its first 10 days (*self-reported*). [AIN](https://en.ain.ua/2022/07/11/ukrainian-united24-platform-raised-13-5m-for-army-of-drones-in-just-10-days)
- **Brave1 launched (April 26, 2023).** The government set up Brave1 as a defence-tech cluster linking developers, the state and the military, with about UAH 100M in starting funding. [AP via News4Jax](https://www.news4jax.com/business/2023/04/26/ukraine-launching-tech-cluster-to-boost-military-capability/)

### 2025
- **Production ramped up.** Officials said monthly drone output went from about 20,000 in early 2024 to about 200,000 by January 2025 (*self-reported*). [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/amp/post/46892)
- **New Brave1 CEO (May 2025).** Andrii Hrytseniuk took over Brave1 with a new team. [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/andrii-hrytseniuk-steps-down-as-head-of-brave1-defense-cluster/)
- **DOT-Chain Defence launched (July 2025).** Front-line units can order drones, ground robots and electronic-warfare kit directly from producers through this state marketplace. [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/10/19/fifteen-months-ago-ukraines-online-arms-store-for-front-line-units-didnt-exist-now-it-delivers-1-in-5-drones-that-state-buys/)
- **Brave1 Market and ePoints (from August 2025).** Units earn points for verified results and spend them on equipment from a catalogue. [Ministry of Defence](https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/troops-have-placed-1-billion-in-orders-in-the-first-year-of-brave1-market)
- **Export plans (November 2025).** Officials announced sales offices in Berlin and Copenhagen and a "Build with Ukraine" joint-production model. A producers' survey found only 14% expected to use their full capacity. [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/65105)

### 2026 to date
- **Interceptor scale-up (January).** Ukraine said it was making about 1,500 interceptor drones a day. Each costs a few thousand dollars, compared with $100K+ for a Shahed (*self-reported*). [UNITED24 Media](https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/how-ukraine-started-2026-with-record-anti-shahed-drone-production-and-a-new-era-in-air-defense-14847)
- **Brave1 Dataroom.** This shared set of combat data for training defence AI had more than 100 companies using it by June. [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/06/11/8038856/)
- **First Ukrainian-rooted defence-tech unicorn (March).** UFORCE, a London-based maker of uncrewed boats founded by Ukrainians, raised $50M at a valuation above $1B. [Vestbee](https://www.vestbee.com/insights/articles/uforce-raises-50-m)
- **Ground robots (April).** The government targeted 25,000 ground robots in the first half of 2026. Brave1 counts about 300 ground-robot companies, up from none in 2022. [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/unmanned/2026/04/24/ukraine-to-field-25000-ground-robots-in-push-to-replace-soldiers-for-frontline-logistics/)
- **Production target (June).** President Zelensky said Ukraine plans about 10 million drones in 2026, with nearly 500 companies in the sector (*self-reported*). [LIGA](https://news.liga.net/en/war/news/zelenskyy-ukraine-will-produce-10-million-drones-this-year)
- **First export of finished combat drones (July 1).** A Ukrainian maker was cleared to ship strike drones to the US. A framework for partner states to buy directly from producers followed. [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/07/03/ukraine-clears-its-first-ever-export-of-finished-combat-drones-and-they-went-to-the-us/)
- **Fedorov dismissed (July 15).** He was removed as defence minister after about six months, and adviser Serhii Sternenko also left the ministry. [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/07/15/fedorov-out-as-ukraines-defense-minister-in-major-government-shake-up/), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/defense-minister-fedorov-set-to-leave-post-senior-official-confirms/)
- **Brave1 passes 1,000 grants (September).** It has given UAH 6.2B since 2023. [Ministry of Defence](https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/brave1-has-awarded-more-than-1-000-grants-to-ukrainian-defense-developers), [The Defense Post](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/09/15/brave1-funding-ukraine-defense-tech/)
- **Buntar Aerospace raises $16M (October 7).** The Kyiv-based reconnaissance-drone maker landed a rare large venture round for a company building inside Ukraine. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/07/ukrainian-defense-tech-buntar-drone)
- **Brave1 leadership exits (October).** Investment chief Artem Moroz left, then CEO Hrytseniuk stepped down on October 19. No successor has been named and the COO is leading during the transition. [Defender Media](https://thedefender.media/en/2026/10/dorozvidka-80/), [Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/more-change-at-brave1-andrii-hrytseniuk-departs-as-ceo-of-ukraines-lauded-defence-cluster/)

## 2. Cyber & OSINT

### Background (2022–2024)
- **IT Army of Ukraine.** This volunteer group formed through public calls at the start of the invasion. Its Telegram channel had more than 231,000 followers by August 2022, and its claims of disrupting Russian services are *self-reported*. [The Record](https://therecord.media/inside-the-it-army-of-ukraine-a-hub-for-digital-resistance)
- **Hackers start passing data to OSINT analysts (April 2024).** A hacker group said it obtained data from a Russian drone developer and gave it to the volunteer OSINT community InformNapalm for analysis (*unverified*). [The Record](https://therecord.media/russia-albatross-drones-alleged-data-leak-ukraine-cyber-resistance)
- **Sanctions expertise.** The Yermak-McFaul expert group, with analysis from KSE Institute, published sanctions action plans in 2022 and 2023. We found no 2026 activity under that name.

### 2025
- **Ukrainian Cyber Alliance and a Russian ISP (January).** The group claimed it took down a Russian internet provider. An outage was observed, and the provider confirmed an attack. The data-wiping claims are *unverified*. [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/172864/hacktivism/ukrainian-cyber-alliance-destroyed-russian-isp-nodex.html)
- **First state award for civilian hackers (March).** Ukraine's military intelligence decorated a civilian hacker group for its work since 2022. [The Record](https://therecord.media/ukraine-intel-service-honors-civilian-hackers-with-military-award)
- **Officials keep their distance (March).** Ukrainian officials acknowledge cooperating with hacktivists but deny directing them. [The Record](https://therecord.media/it-army-keeps-up-attacks-on-russia-ukraine)
- **Russian court records (May).** A pro-Ukraine group claimed an attack on Russia's court filing system. A Russian audit report later said about a third of the case archive was lost. The group's role is *claimed, not confirmed*. [The Record](https://therecord.media/russia-court-system-hack-third-of-case-files-deleted)
- **Aeroflot outage (July).** Ukrainian and Belarusian hacktivists claimed an attack that grounded more than 100 Aeroflot flights. Russian prosecutors confirmed a cyberattack, but the scale claims are *unverified*. [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/180521/hacktivism/pro-ukraine-hacktivists-claim-cyberattack-on-russian-airline-aeroflot-that-caused-the-cancellation-of-100-flights.html)
- **Cyber Forces bill (October).** Parliament passed bill 12349 in first reading. It would create military cyber forces with a civilian "cyber reservist" route. [Ukrinform](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-polytics/4045484-parliament-approves-creation-of-ukraines-military-cyber-forces.html)
- **Leaked Russian defence-industry documents (November).** InformNapalm and partners published documents from a Russian defence supplier. The supplier was later added to an EU sanctions package. The documents are *not independently verified*. [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/leak-of-secret-russian-defense-industry-documents-informnapalm-publishes-data-on-the-poslannik-pak-da-and-su-57/)
- **DeepState front-line map.** This donation-funded map is widely treated as the main independent view of the front line. Commanders have questioned how current it is. [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/the-blurred-front-line-of-ukraines-drone-dominated-battlefield-is-making-mapping-harder-and-more-political/), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/02/10/nx-s1-5275976/ukrainians-are-closely-following-a-homegrown-map-of-the-shifting-front-line)

### 2026 to date
- **Cyber volunteers' legal status is still unresolved.** OSINT volunteers accused lawmakers of stalling bill 12349. A civilian component was added in March, but the bill had not passed its second reading as of June. We found no vote by October 19. [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/70756), [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/ukraine-access-to-eu-cybersecurity-reserve/)
- **Shadow-fleet tracking.** KSE Institute's monthly tracker found the share of shadow tankers flying the Russian flag rose from 3% to 21% between May 2025 and February 2026. [KSE](https://kse.ua/about-the-school/news/russian-shadow-fleet-tracker-march-2026-share-of-russian-flagged-shadow-tankers-jumps-from-3-to-21-in-nine-months/)
- **Russian satellite-communications data (April).** OSINT and hacker communities said they obtained internal data from a Russian satellite system and handed it to Ukraine's defence forces (*unverified*). [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/ukrainian-hackers-breach-internal-data-of-russia-s-starlink-like-gonets-system/)
- **NGO analysis of missile debris (July).** The anti-corruption NGO NAKO reported that Russia's Oreshnik missile relies heavily on Russian and Belarusian semiconductors. [Business & Human Rights Resource Centre](https://www.business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/Ukraine-NAKO-report-analyses-components-of-Oreshnik-missile-used-in-attacks-on-Ukraine-revealing-reliance-on-Russian-Belarusian-semiconductors/)
- **The line between civic and state action is blurring (August).** Some 2026 "volunteer" operations were announced by military intelligence rather than by the groups themselves, which makes them harder to class as civilian. [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/cyberattack-wildberries-ukrainian-hackers/)
- **Russian naval documents (September).** A hacker community said it obtained documentation on dozens of Russian naval projects. Authenticity is *unverified*. [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/ukrainian-hackers-claim-to-have-obtained-documentation-on-70-russian-naval-projects/)

## 3. Civilian apps & AI tools

### Background (2022–2024)
- **ePPO (autumn 2022).** This app lets citizens report missiles and drones after logging in through Diia. Its developer says it has saved "hundreds of lives" (*self-reported*). [Mediacenter](https://mediacenter.org.ua/eppo-app-has-been-in-operation-for-a-year-hundreds-of-ukrainian-lives-saved/)
- **AI for demining priorities (October 2023).** The Economy Ministry presented an AI-supported strategy for deciding which suspected minefields to clear first. [Ministry of Economy](https://me.gov.ua/News/Detail?lang=en-GB&id=db1e15c6-b9f4-43ec-88b9-7a1bcf336e5a&title=ArtificialIntelligenceToHelpDeminingUkraine)
- **Diia passes 20 million users (January 2024).** [Government of Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/derzhava-u-smartfoni-diieiu-korystuietsia-ponad-20-milioniv-ukraintsiv)

### 2025
- **War-crimes evidence app (February).** Ukrainian users had captured more than 53,000 items through eyeWitness to Atrocities. A coalition of more than 30 NGOs uses it, and some evidence has reached Ukrainian courts. [dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/53000-tsyfrovykh-dokaziv-deiaki-z-iakykh-vzhe-v-sudakh-yak-zastosunok-eyewitness-to-atrocities-fiksuie-voienni-zlochyny-rf-i-zakhyshchaie-tykh-khto-nym-korystuietsia-1739962240)
- **AI test site for mine action (February).** Officials and UNDP began planning a test site for AI in demining. About 156,000 km² may be contaminated. [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/02/25/7500107)
- **eVorog chatbot (March).** This chatbot for civilians reporting enemy movements had received more than 660,000 reports since 2022 (*self-reported*). [Ukrinform](https://ukrinform.net/rubric-defense/3974029-ukrainians-actively-helping-army-via-enemy-spotting-chatbot-vice-pm.html)
- **eVidnovlennia home repairs (May).** More than 100,000 families had received repair money or housing certificates through this Diia-based programme. [Ministry of Infrastructure](https://mininfra.gov.ua/en/news/2-roky-ievidnovlennia-ponad-100-tysiach-simei-otrymaly-kompensatsii)
- **Diia.AI launches (September).** The ministry called it the world's first state-run AI agent (its own claim). It launched in open beta on the web portal. [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/09/01/7528804/)

### 2026 to date
- **Fedorov moves to defence (January).** He left the digital ministry to become defence minister. [The Record](https://therecord.media/ukraine-digital-chief-transformation)
- **Air Alert app (January).** It has more than 46 million downloads and 6.6 million active users (*self-reported* by the developer, Ajax). [dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/povitriana-tryvoha-1768230553)
- **Register of Damage (January).** The international register reached 100,000 individual claims, filed through Diia. [Council of Europe](https://rd4u.coe.int/en/-/register-of-damage-receives-100-000-claims-submitted-by-individuals-affected-by-russia-s-aggression-against-ukraine)
- **SvitloDim backup-power grants (January onward).** Apartment buildings can apply through Diia for UAH 100,000–300,000 for backup power. 2,442 buildings had been funded by July, and four more regions were added on October 14. [Government of Ukraine](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/yuliia-svyrydenko-uriad-zapuskaie-svitlodim-prohramu-pidtrymky-bahatokvartyrnykh-budynkiv-dlia-avtonomnoho-elektrozhyvlennia), [Interfax-Ukraine](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/economic/1219784.html)
- **"Agentic state" strategy (February).** The digital ministry made AI a core layer of government and planned a national language model. Its training is waiting on rules for using public and consented data. [Digital State](https://digitalstate.gov.ua/news/govtech/nastupnyy-etap-tsyfrovoyi-derzavy-shi-defenstek-ta-upravlinnia)
- **AI disinformation (April).** Ukraine's Center for Countering Disinformation found more than 1,000 AI-generated videos in one coordinated Russian campaign. Fact-checkers now routinely use AI-detection tools. [Ukrinform](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/4115994-russia-expands-ai-disinformation-into-cognitive-warfare.html), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/fact-check-russia-turns-to-ai-to-push-fakes-about-womens-draft-in-ukraine-2/)
- **Diia.AI moves into the app and adds voice (May and July).** The AI agent launched in the Diia mobile app on May 14 and added voice conversations on July 21. [Babel](https://babel.ua/en/news/126979-diia-launched-its-own-ai-agent-in-the-application-what-services-does-it-support), [Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/ukraine-expands-diia-ai-with-voice-based-access-to-government-services)
- **New digital minister (July 16).** Oksana Ferchuk was appointed and is pitching Ukraine as an "AI sandbox" for Europe. [Interfax-Ukraine](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/general/1185591.html), [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/29/ukraines-digital-minister-we-want-europe-to-use-ukraine-as-an-ai-sandbox)
- **Drone munitions and demining (October).** A UN adviser warned that unexploded ordnance from drones could reach historic scale. AI image analysis is being used in mine surveys. [The Defense Post](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/10/15/drone-warfare-ukraine-explosive-hazards/)

## 4. Crowdfunding & hardware

### Background (2022–2024)
- **People's Bayraktar becomes a satellite contract (2022).** The Prytula Foundation raised about UAH 600M in three days to buy Bayraktar drones. The manufacturer then donated the drones, and the money bought radar satellite access for the armed forces instead. [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/08/18/7363829)
- **UNITED24 takes off (July 2022).** The government fundraising platform passed $75M from 90 countries in its first two months. [Rubryka](https://rubryka.com/en/2022/07/10/united24-zvit-2-misyatsi/)
- **Jar giving peaks (2024).** Donations through monobank jars hit a record in 2024, while large foundations reported falling military donations. [Rubryka](https://rubryka.com/en/2024/12/08/zsu/)

### 2025
- **Interceptor fundraising begins (January).** The Prytula Foundation launched a drive for 600 interceptor drones against reconnaissance UAVs. [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/serhiy-prytula-charity-foundation-launches-fundraiser-for-600-interceptor-drones/)
- **Come Back Alive's record foreign grant (October).** The foundation received about UAH 11.6B (~$272M) in grants from undisclosed international partners. [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/10/30/8005105)
- **Donor fatigue (November).** Fundraisers that once closed in days now take weeks, and organisers are turning to gamification, merch and influencer campaigns. [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/articles/2025/11/13/8007158/)
- **Largest diaspora defence fundraiser (November).** The Ukrainian World Congress and the Prytula Foundation launched a UAH 1B drive for anti-drone systems. [UWC](https://www.ukrainianworldcongress.org/uwc-prytula-foundation-and-412th-nemesis-brigade-unite-in-ukraines-largest-ever-defense-fundraiser-for-anti-drone-systems/)
- **2025 totals (December).** Jar donations fell 19% year on year. UNITED24, Come Back Alive and Prytula together raised UAH 105.9B, with UNITED24 at a record. [Opendatabot](https://opendatabot.ua/en/analytics/donats-2025)

### 2026 to date
- **Come Back Alive's 2025 total (January).** Its website shows UAH 26.83B raised in 2025, against UAH 4.58B in 2024. Its director cited a higher figure. The 2026 goal combines $1B from foreign governments with steady domestic giving. [Interfax-Ukraine](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/1139547.html)
- **Diaspora tour (March).** A North American tour by the Ukrainian World Congress and the Prytula Foundation raised UAH 102.5M for anti-Shahed capability. [UWC](https://www.ukrainianworldcongress.org/uwc-and-prytula-foundation-announce-conclusion-of-one-united-fundraiser-2026-north-american-tour-raising-over-102-5-million-uah-to-protect-ukrainian-civilians/)
- **Giving behaviour (March).** 62% of Ukrainians donated or volunteered in 2025, down from 86% in 2024, but more of those who give now give regularly. 52% cite distrust of foundations. [VoxUkraine](https://voxukraine.org/en/how-charity-has-changed-compared-with-2024-a-study-by-zagoriy-foundation)
- **Volunteer 3D-printing networks (April).** Online networks match requests from units with volunteer printer operators. Reported output is large, but the figures come from a single report. [Fabbaloo, citing ABC](https://www.fabbaloo.com/news/secret-decentralized-3d-printing-networks-supply-drone-parts-to-ukraine-at-scale)
- **UNITED24 quarter (July).** The platform raised $89.5M from April to July 2026, with diaspora and creator campaigns funding interceptors and ground robots. [UNITED24](https://u24.gov.ua/news/3-9-milyarda-gryven-vdalosa-zibraty-zavdyaky-donoram-i-partneram-united24)
- **UNITED24 total (August).** Zelensky said the platform has raised nearly $4B since 2022 from 145 countries. [Mezha](https://mezha.net/eng/news/f59d997c_zelenskyy_says_united24/)
- **monobank jar statistics (August).** Jars have taken in more than UAH 143B since 2022, and Sternenko's Community foundation leads charities in jar collections (*self-reported*). [Kurs](https://kurs.com.ua/en/novost/1475551-sternenko-vozglavil-reiting-fondov-po-sboram-cherez-monobank)
- **Prytula strike-drone campaign (September).** The UAH 1.3B campaign was more than 80% funded, and 135 drones had been delivered. [LB.ua](https://en.lb.ua/news/2026/09/18/42206_serhiy_prytula_foundation.html)
- **Operation Interception (October 14).** Kyivstar and the Sternenko Community Foundation launched a UAH 100M drive for interceptors against jet-powered Shaheds. A day later, Come Back Alive delivered 80 test jet interceptors. [Censor.net](https://censor.net/en/news/4028379/kyivstar-and-the-sternenko-foundation-have-announced-a-fundraising-campaign-details), [LB.ua](https://en.lb.ua/news/2026/10/15/45300_come_back_alive_delivers_80_jet.html)
